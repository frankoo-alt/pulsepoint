⚠️ Clinical Problem Statement

The Cost of Fragmented Healthcare

Seconds save lives, yet clinical logistics operate on antiquated, disconnected silos.

📞

Manual Telephone Coordination

When Level 1 trauma victims need rare units (like O- or B-), hospitals lose precious hours calling neighboring facilities one-by-one to confirm stock.

🏃‍♂️

Prescription Run-Around

Patients and caregivers visit 4 to 6 local chemists during emergencies only to find critical antibiotics, insulin, or inhalers out of stock.

🛡️

Data Privacy & Public Opacity

Existing systems fail to separate public transparency from private clinical security, risking donor PII leaks and cold-chain vault tampering.

🌡️

Cold-Chain Chain-of-Custody Gaps

Blood products must be maintained strictly between 2°C and 6°C. Current inter-hospital couriers lack cryptographic temperature-logged transfer passes.

Market Need: 42% of avoidable trauma delays stem from manual inventory verification. Slide 2 of 8

🚀 Core Hackathon Innovation

Inter-Facility Transfer & Cold-Chain Stepper

Cryptographically verified inter-hospital blood requisitions and real-time cold-chain tracking.

5-Stage Logistics Pipeline Stepper

1
Pending Review: Requisition raised by surgeon with clinical rationale.
2
Stock Matched: Automated algorithm allocates units from nearest repository.
3
Courier Dispatched: Cryo-box packaged with calibrated sensors.
4
In Transit: Live temperature probes monitor compliance (3.8°C - 4.2°C).
5
Delivered & Cryo-Stored: Safely received at destination blood bank.

🔒 Security & Cold-Chain Guarantees

  • 🔑 Medical Passkey Gatekeeper: Hospital credentials verified (`MED-AUTH-9082`).
  • 📦 Cryptographic SHA Token: Every shipment generates an immutable tracking token (e.g. `PP-TX-7994-O-NEG-COLD-CHAIN-VERIFIED-9812A`).
  • ❄️ Cold-Chain Vault Monitoring: Unmasked cryo-tank rack codes, pathologist clearance sign-offs, and live inventory `+`/`-` steppers.
Regulatory: 2°C to 6°C blood storage compliance verified throughout courier transit. Slide 7 of 8
